# Premium bilingual eye clinic website

## What will be built
- Replace the blank page with a polished, mobile-first eye clinic website in English and Hindi.
- Add a sticky header, language switcher, accessible text-size controls, desktop navigation, mobile menu, and persistent call/WhatsApp booking actions.
- Build the requested homepage flow: hero, trust signals, founder profile, services, why choose us, cataract care, technology, team, patient journey, testimonials, FAQs, appointment call-to-action, contact details, map area, and footer.
- Use clearly labelled placeholders for the clinic name, doctor credentials, contact details, address, hours, equipment, statistics, and testimonials until verified information is supplied.

## Visual direction
- Light, spacious private-clinic aesthetic with midnight navy, medical blue, aqua, white, and restrained champagne details.
- Layered doctor/eye imagery, subtle depth, gentle motion, and polished cards without excessive gradients or glass effects.
- Large, high-contrast typography and generous touch targets suited to elderly and visually impaired visitors.

## Interaction and accessibility
- Natural Hindi translations across all visible content.
- Language switching without reload, keyboard-friendly FAQ accordions, responsive mobile menu, readable controls, semantic structure, ARIA labels, and reduced-motion support.
- WhatsApp links with prefilled English or Hindi appointment details; call and directions links remain easy to replace once real details are provided.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing TanStack Start, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind setup.
- Create reusable presentation components and centralized bilingual/configuration data.
- Add route-level search metadata and MedicalClinic, Physician, FAQ, and Breadcrumb structured data using placeholders only where facts are unknown.
- Preserve crawler access. A new sitemap will be deferred until a public website address exists.
- Validate the desktop and mobile layouts in the running preview and check current build diagnostics.
